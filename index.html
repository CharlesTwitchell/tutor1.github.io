How sessions work

Same brain, better scaffolding.

Every student's ADHD shows up differently, so every plan starts from scratch — but the sessions always follow a rhythm designed to work with a wandering brain instead of against it.

Step 1

Body double & warm-up

We start together, out loud, so starting the work is never the hardest part of the work.

Step 2

Break it down

Big assignments get cut into steps small enough that "I don't know where to start" stops being true.

Step 3

Focus sprints

Short timed sprints with real breaks in between — the dial above is exactly what we use live.
