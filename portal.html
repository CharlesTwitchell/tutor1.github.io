Setup note (remove before publishing): videos aren't actually stored anywhere in this demo — courses you add here disappear on refresh, and the file picker doesn't upload. For a real portal you have two good paths: (1) easiest — host your videos on Vimeo (with its privacy/domain-restriction settings) or YouTube (unlisted) and embed the player per course, using this page purely as a catalog; (2) more control — use a course platform like Teachable, Thinkific, or Kajabi for hosting, drip-scheduling, and student logins, and link out to it from here. If you want students to log in and track progress on your own site specifically, that also needs real user accounts — a developer can add this with a service like Supabase or Firebase Auth.
